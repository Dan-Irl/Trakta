# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Start with the FAQ on [trakta.app](https://trakta.app), then ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/Dan-Irl/Trakta/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Dan-Irl/Trakta/issues/new/choose).
- **An amount looks wrong:** open a "Wrong amount or rule" issue and link the Skatteverket page that shows the right answer.
- **Anything private:** email hello@trakta.app.

Issues and discussions are public, so leave out names, addresses, registration numbers and other personal data.

Trakta is not tax advice and has no connection to Skatteverket. For questions about your own tax return, ask [Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se) directly.
