# Changelog

User-visible changes in each version of Trakta. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/). A version's section becomes its GitHub release notes, so write for users.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.1.0]

### Added

- Trakta is open source under the Apache License 2.0.
- The Android app can be downloaded straight from [trakta.app](https://trakta.app/ladda-ner/), signed and with a checksum and build provenance for every release.
- Settings shows the version, where updates come from, a link to the source code and the licences of the packages the app uses.

## [1.0.0]

First test release, on Google Play's internal testing track.

- Per diem (traktamente) for business trips in Sweden and abroad, day by day, with meals, lodging and what the employer paid.
- Driving log (körjournal) for business driving with your own or a company car.
- Commuting deduction (reseavdrag) from a weekly schedule, with costs and the parking rule.
- Overview of the expected deduction for the income year, with a "Why?" for every amount.
- Documents per trip, drive and commuting cost, stored only on the phone.
- Encrypted backup and restore, between Android and iPhone.
- Swedish and English.
