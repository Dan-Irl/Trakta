The code in this repository is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): you may use, change and share it. The licence covers copyright only. Section 6 of the licence says it does not grant permission to use the project's name or marks, and this policy explains what you can do with them.

**The marks:** the name **Trakta**, the Trakta logo and the app icon (`design/app_icon*.png` and the icons generated from them).

## Why

Trakta tells people how much they can deduct on their Swedish tax return. Anyone who sees "Trakta" should be able to trust that it is this app, built from this repository, with rules checked against Skatteverket's published amounts. A modified copy with different rules, ads or tracking under the same name would mislead them.

## Fine without asking

- Saying that something is **based on**, **a fork of** or **compatible with** Trakta, or writing about Trakta (reviews, articles, talks, tutorials).
- Linking to trakta.app or this repository with the name or icon.
- Sharing **unmodified official builds** (from trakta.app or this repository's GitHub releases), including in app catalogues such as F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid or Obtainium, under the name Trakta.
- Building the app from this repository for your own use.

## Not without written permission

- Publishing a **modified** app, website or service under the name Trakta, a confusingly similar name, the logo or the app icon.
- Implying that your project is official, endorsed by or affiliated with Trakta.
- Using the marks in a company, product or domain name.

## If you fork

Pick your own name and icon, and change the app ID (`applicationId` in `apps/trakta_app/android/app/build.gradle.kts` and `PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER` in `apps/trakta_app/ios/Runner.xcodeproj`), so your app can be installed next to Trakta and is never mistaken for it. You keep everything else the licence gives you.

## Questions

Ask at hello@trakta.app. Trakta has no connection to Skatteverket, and its own name and logo must not suggest one either.
